## Graph Construction

Extract entities dan relationships dari document corpus. Build knowledge graph.

## Retrieval Strategy

Hybrid: vector search untuk semantic, graph traversal untuk relationship.

## Use Cases

Medical knowledge, legal reasoning, enterprise search.
