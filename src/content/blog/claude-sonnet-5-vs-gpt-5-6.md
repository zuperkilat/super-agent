## Feature Comparison

Claude Sonnet 5: 200K context, tool use, computer use. GPT-5.6: 1M context, reasoning modes, multimodal.

## Benchmark Results

Claude unggul di coding dan reasoning. GPT-5.6 unggul di large document analysis dan vision.

## Pricing

Claude Sonnet: $3/1M in, $15/1M out. GPT-5.6: $2.5/1M in, $10/1M out. Perbedaan 20-30%.
