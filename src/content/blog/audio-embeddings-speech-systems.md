## Audio Embedding Models

Whisper, Wav2Vec2, HuBERT. Encode audio into latent representations.

## Use Cases

Speaker identification, audio classification, speech retrieval.

## Multimodal Fusion

Combine audio embedding dengan text dan image embeddings for multi-modal agents.
