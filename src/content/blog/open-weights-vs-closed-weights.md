## Open Weights Advantages

Data privacy (on-prem deployment), custom fine-tuning, cost control, no vendor lock-in.

## Closed Weights Advantages

State-of-art performance tanpa training cost, managed infrastructure, automatic updates.

## Decision Framework

Data sensitivity high -> open weights. Time-to-market critical -> closed weights. Need customization -> open weights or fine-tune closed.
