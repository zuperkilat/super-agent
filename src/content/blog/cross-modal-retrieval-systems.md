## Shared Embedding Space

CLIP-style training: contrastive learning antar modalities. Image-text, text-audio pairs.

## Retrieval Patterns

Text-to-image, image-to-text, audio-to-text. Universal encoder for all modalities.

## Industrial Applications

E-commerce visual search, accessibility tools, content recommendation.
