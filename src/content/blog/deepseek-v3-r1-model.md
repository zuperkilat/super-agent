## DeepSeek V3

Mixture-of-Experts model dengan 671B total params, 37B active. Training cost ~$5.5M.

## DeepSeek R1

Reasoning-specific variant. Chain-of-thought fine-tuning. Benchmark competitive dengan OpenAI o1.

## Open Source Impact

DeepSeek membuktikan model canggih bisa di-training dengan cost relatif murah.impact pada open-source community.
