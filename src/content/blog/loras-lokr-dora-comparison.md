## LoRA

Decompose weight update jadi low-rank matrices. Rank 8-16 usually cukup. Hemat 75% VRAM.

## LoKr

Kronecker product untuk even fewer params. Good untuk extreme compression.

## DoRA

Decompose magnitude dan direction. Better convergence.
