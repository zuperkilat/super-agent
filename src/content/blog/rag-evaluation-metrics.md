## Faithfulness

Apakah response didukung oleh retrieved documents. Hitungan: claim extraction + evidence matching.

## Answer Relevancy

Apakah response menjawab question. Cosine similarity between question dan answer embedding.

## Context Precision/Recall

Precision: retrieved relevant / total retrieved. Recall: retrieved relevant / total relevant.
