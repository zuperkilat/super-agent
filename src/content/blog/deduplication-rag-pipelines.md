## Exact Deduplication

Hash-based: extract chunk, compute SHA256, filter duplicates. Simple tapi miss near-duplicates.

## Near-Deduplication

MinHash untuk approximate similarity. Locality Sensitive Hashing.

## Semantic Deduplication

Compute embedding, filter by cosine similarity > 0.95. Remove redundant information.
